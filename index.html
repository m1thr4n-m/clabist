What is CLab?

CLab is the SRM IST virtual chemistry laboratory. It reads live records from a large open chemical database and pairs them with a periodic table, a short course and a graded quiz.

Read the notes, watch the five videos and tick each one. The quiz opens when all five are ticked.

Elements follow a pattern

Effective nuclear charge rises across a period, so atoms shrink and ionization energy increases. Down a group, extra shells shield the nucleus, so radius grows and ionization energy falls.

Molecules as text

SMILES writes a molecule as a string of atoms and bonds. Aspirin is CC(=O)OC1=CC=CC=C1C(=O)O. SDF files store 3D coordinates.

Lipinski’s rule of five

Oral drugs usually have mass up to 500, logP up to 5, no more than 5 hydrogen bond donors and 10 acceptors. One violation is tolerated.

Seeing 3D

Ball and stick shows connectivity. Space filling shows van der Waals volume. Force fields such as MMFF94 relax bond lengths and angles.
